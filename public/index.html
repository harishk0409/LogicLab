Operator guide

Use these operators to write or compare logical expressions.

NOT P · ¬P · !PNegation
P AND Q · P ∧ Q · P && QBoth true
P OR Q · P ∨ Q · P || QEither true
P XOR Q · P ⊕ QExactly one true
P → Q · P -> QImplication
P ↔ Q · P <-> QEquivalent

Precedence: NOT → AND → XOR → OR → IMPLIES → IFF. Parentheses override the order. Constants: TRUE, FALSE, 1, 0.
